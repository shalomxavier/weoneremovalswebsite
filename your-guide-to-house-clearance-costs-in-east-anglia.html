Your Guide to House Clearance Costs in East Anglia

  1. Admin
  2. 23 Feb 2025

Clearing a home in East Anglia – whether for a fresh start, downsizing, or just decluttering – often begins with one big question in mind: "What's this going to cost me?" Here is a guide to help you out. Check out how factors like the volume and type of items, location, and property access, among others, shape your house clearance quote.

Average House Clearance Costs

In the UK, a typical house clearance can range anywhere from £175 for a quarter van load to upwards of £1,000 for a larger, full property clearance.

A part clearance (extra small) might start from £150. For instance, for a 1-bedroom flat, clearance can start from £200, while for a 3-bedroom house, it might be anywhere from £250 upward.

(https://www.checkatrade.com/blog/cost-guides/house-clearance-cost/)

The precise house clearance quote will always depend on specific details, such as the location, the items to be removed, part clearance or full clearance, their condition, and whether they can be recycled, among others. In East Anglia (including Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire, and Essex), prices generally align with national averages.

There is no one-size-fits-all pricing, because no two clearances are ever truly identical. There will almost always be additional charges for disposing of or recycling certain items, such as household appliances, building supplies, and large items.

What Influences Your Quote?

So, what actually goes into that final figure? There are several key factors to take into account when your quote is prepared.

Volume of items

Naturally, the more you have, the more it costs. The sheer volume of items needing clearance directly affects the time, labour, and vehicle space required.

Clearing standard household items like old furniture and clothing is straightforward. However, certain items can incur additional charges due to specific disposal regulations, environmental fees, or the need for specialised handling (think fridges or pianos!).

The weight and bulkiness of goods also play a role, as heavier, more awkward items demand greater effort to remove.

On the flip side, if there are items in good, reusable condition, that can sometimes make a positive difference in your clearance quote. At WeOne, we always aim to recycle and resell furniture where possible.

Type of Items

The types of items you need cleared also significantly influence the overall cost.

While everyday household clutter is generally straightforward, certain "problem items" come with extra charges. Large appliances like fridges and freezers, old televisions, or mattresses require specialised handling and disposal due to environmental regulations.

Now, here is a bonus with WeOne. We factor in items that can be refurbished or resold. If your clearance includes valuable or reusable goods, we might offer a discount, offsetting the cost and ensuring you get an even fairer price.

Property Size & Accessibility

The size of your property also impacts the overall cost. A larger property typically means more items and thus a higher cost.. Then there are other factors that affect the price.

Are items on the upper floors without a lift? Is there a long walk from the property to where the van can park? Are there permits required to park near the property?

All of these can influence the pricing on the quote.

Location within East Anglia:

While the rate across East Anglia (Norfolk, Suffolk, Cambridgeshire, Essex) is almost the same, very remote locations might incur slightly higher fuel or travel time costs.

Time and Labour Required:

The longer a clearance takes and the more team members are required, the higher the labour cost.

More items, especially if they are heavy or bulky, naturally require more staff and longer hours to sort, lift, and load safely. Similarly, properties with challenging access, such as those with many stairs, no nearby parking, or narrow doorways, will extend the time our team spends on-site.

At WeOne, we simplify things by charging straightforward hourly rates for local moves up to 30 miles. Also, you only pay for the actual distance your goods travel, not from our depot.

Larger or more complex jobs, which may include clearances, always receive a customised, personalised quote.

Why do you have to pay extra for some goods?

Even with all this breakdown, you will still find some numbers that pop into your quote. There is a reason for the hidden extras.

Some items cannot simply go to a general waste facility as they require specific recycling or disposal processes, leading to extra charges. These commonly include:

  • Fridges and Freezers: Due to hazardous coolants. Price varies depending on the size of the fridge.
  • Televisions and Monitors (WEEE Waste): Electrical and electronic equipment requires special recycling.
  • Smaller Electrical Items: There is also an additional fee here, due to the need for special recycling.
  • Mattresses: An additional price applies for disposing of mattresses, as they are difficult to compact and recycle.
  • Heavy Goods: Heavy goods, such as pianos, are extremely heavy and often require more labour effort and specialist equipment.

What's Included in Your WeOne Quote?

When you receive a quote from WeOne Removals and Clearances Ltd., we aim for absolute clarity. We itemize all additional pricing transparently, so you always know exactly what you're paying for.

Our standard quotes typically include:

  • All Labour: Our experienced team will handle all the heavy lifting, sorting, and loading of items.
  • Transport: The cost of the vehicle and fuel required to transport your items from your property to the appropriate disposal or recycling facilities.
  • Standard Disposal Fees: This covers the cost of legally and responsibly disposing of general waste at licensed transfer stations.
