Answers to your moving questions

Yes, WeOne provides a complete dismantling and reassembly service for furniture. The skilled team ensures that all furniture is carefully taken apart and correctly put back together at the new location.

WeOne's vans are fully equipped with all the necessary safety measures. These include straps, sack barrows, furniture blankets, floor protectors, and other equipment to protect belongings. Customers also have the option to add services like shrink-wrapping for an extra layer of protection.

The professionals at WeOne are specially trained to handle fragile items with the utmost care. The moving vans are fully equipped with straps, sack barrows, blankets, and other safety measures to ensure the secure transport of valuable items like pianos, antiques, and valuable art.

It is good practice for clients to label boxes to easily identify fragile items or those requiring special care. They should also prepare appliances for packing and have labelled bags for storing nuts and screws from disassembled furniture.
