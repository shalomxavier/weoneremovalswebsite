Professional House Clearance Services

Whether you're clearing a property after a bereavement, preparing a home for sale, downsizing, or simply making space, We One Removals provides professional house clearance services across East Anglia. We clear everything from individual rooms and garages to complete properties, always working respectfully, efficiently, and with care for both your property and the items inside it.

Operating since 2016, we're fully insured and hold a Higher Tier Waste Carrier Licence with the UK Environment Agency. Wherever possible, suitable items are recycled or donated to charity, helping us achieve a 94–96% recycling and reuse rate while reducing the amount sent to landfill. If your property contains valuable or saleable items, we can also assess them during the quotation, and where applicable, their agreed value may be offset against your house clearance cost.

How Our Clearance Process Works

01

Quote & Assessment

Getting started is simple. Contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or request a property visit. We'll discuss your requirements and provide a free quotation based on the size of the clearance and the items involved.

02

Valuable Items & Cost Offset

During the assessment, we can identify valuable or saleable items. Where applicable, an agreed value for suitable items may be deducted from or offset against your house clearance cost.

03

Booking Your Clearance

Once you're happy with the quotation, we'll arrange a convenient date and time. If you need urgent assistance, we also offer same-day emergency clearance whenever availability allows.

04

Professional Clearance

Our team arrives on time and carries out the clearance efficiently and respectfully. Items are carefully sorted for recycling, reuse, charity donation where appropriate, or responsible disposal through licensed facilities.

05

Swept Clean & Ready

Before we leave, we make sure the property is left swept clean and ready for its next stage, whether that's preparing for sale, welcoming new tenants, or simply giving you one less thing to worry about.

Situations We Help With

Bereavement & Probate

Clearing the home of a loved one can be emotionally difficult. We work with care, patience, and respect, helping families, executors, and solicitors clear properties professionally while allowing you the time and space you need.

Downsizing and Moving

Moving to a smaller property often means deciding what to keep, move, donate, or clear. We help make the transition easier by efficiently clearing unwanted furniture and household items, leaving the property ready for its next stage.

Garage, Loft & Shed Clearance

Garages, lofts, and sheds often become home to years of accumulated belongings. We clear these spaces safely, removing unwanted items so they're clean, practical, and ready to use again.

Hoarding Situations

Every situation is different. Our team approaches hoarding clearances without judgement, working respectfully and discreetly while helping restore properties safely and responsibly.

Landlord & Estate Agent

Whether you're preparing a property for new tenants or getting a home ready for sale, we provide reliable clearance services that help properties become market-ready as quickly as possible.

End of Tenancy

Leaving a rented property? We remove unwanted furniture, household items, and general clutter, leaving the property cleared and swept clean for handover.
