frequently asked questions

Answers to your moving questions

WeOne is equipped to handle most household and commercial goods.

However, for reasons of safety and UK legal regulations, certain items cannot be transported. These include illegal goods, hazardous materials such as flammable liquids, and anything that requires a special license for transport.

WeOne is fully covered with all the necessary insurance to provide peace of mind to its customers. This includes Goods in Transit insurance up to £50,000 and Public Liability insurance up to £5 million, ensuring that belongings are protected throughout the entire process.

WeOne recommends that clients book their move at least two to three weeks in advance. This ensures a smooth and well-planned experience. However, last-minute requests can be accommodated, depending on the availability of resources.

The duration of a move is entirely dependent on the size and nature of the job. A smaller flat relocation or a partial removal might take only a few hours, while a larger house will likely require an entire day.

WeOne offers personalised moving plans and pricing to suit each client's specific needs.
