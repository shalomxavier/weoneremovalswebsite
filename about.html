About us

Setting new standards in the UK removals industry

Our journey began in 2016 with our founder, Mr. Chris. He recognised the immense stress and overwhelm people faced during house clearances and relocations. Chris envisioned a company that went beyond simple logistics. He wanted to offer a service that cared for the people, too. That's how WeOne Removals and Clearances Ltd. came to be.

Fast forward to 2025, our hardworking team is proud to have completed over 8,000 jobs and counting. Our commitment to the kind of service we provide, combined with a 96% recycling rate, means you gain a clear space, peace of mind, and the best rate, too.

Today, WeOne Removals and Clearances Ltd is a dedicated removal and house clearance company committed to hassle-free relocations across the UK.

“When I started WeOne Removals in 2016, my goal was simple: to make moving less of a headache and more of a fresh start. We've grown since then, completing over 8,000 jobs, and every single one has been about delivering on that commitment.”

Chris

Founder, WeOne Removals & Clearances Ltd

Trusted for nearly a decade of expert removals service.
